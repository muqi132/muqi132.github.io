一、 数列极限的计算方法

1. 夹逼定理（求和式极限）

例1：求 lim⁡n→∞(13n2+2n+1+23n2+2n+2+⋯+n3n2+2n+n)\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{1}{3n^2+2n+1} + \frac{2}{3n^2+2n+2} + \dots + \frac{n}{3n^2+2n+n} \right)limn→∞​(3n2+2n+11​+3n2+2n+22​+⋯+3n2+2n+nn​)

  • 解析：令原式为 xnx_nxn​。利用放缩法，将分母统一放大或缩小。
    • 分母全部取最大（3n2+2n+n3n^2+2n+n3n2+2n+n），则和式最小：1+2+⋯+n3n2+2n+n≤xn\frac{1+2+\dots+n}{3n^2+2n+n} \le x_n3n2+2n+n1+2+⋯+n​≤xn​
    • 分母全部取最小（3n2+2n+13n^2+2n+13n2+2n+1），则和式最大：xn≤1+2+⋯+n3n2+2n+1x_n \le \frac{1+2+\dots+n}{3n^2+2n+1}xn​≤3n2+2n+11+2+⋯+n​
  • 化简两端：12n(n+1)3n2+2n+n=12n2+12n3n2+3n\frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{3n^2+2n+n} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}n^2 + \frac{1}{2}n}{3n^2+3n}3n2+2n+n21​n(n+1)​=3n2+3n21​n2+21​n​ 与 12n(n+1)3n2+2n+1\frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{3n^2+2n+1}3n2+2n+121​n(n+1)​。
  • 求极限：lim⁡n→∞12n(n+1)3n2+2n+n=16\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{3n^2+2n+n} = \frac{1}{6}limn→∞​3n2+2n+n21​n(n+1)​=61​，lim⁡n→∞12n(n+1)3n2+2n+1=16\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{3n^2+2n+1} = \frac{1}{6}limn→∞​3n2+2n+121​n(n+1)​=61​。
